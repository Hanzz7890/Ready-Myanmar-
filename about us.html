About Ready Myanmar

Ready Myanmar helps people understand the hazards where they live, learn from past disasters, and take practical steps to protect their homes and communities.

Meet the team Contact us

Our mission, vision, and values

Our mission

Make disaster risk information easy to understand and give households practical ways to reduce harm.

Our vision

Communities across Myanmar where people know their local risks, have a plan, and know how to help one another.

Our values

Use clear language, show where our facts come from, respect people affected by disasters, and give advice people can use.

Our aims and objectives

Four ways we help people move from understanding a hazard to acting on it.

  1. Build awareness

    Explain what causes earthquakes, landslides, subsidence and sinkholes, tsunamis, and floods.

  2. Make risk local

    Show where hazards have affected people in Myanmar and help readers explore the places that matter to them.

  3. Show how to reduce harm

    Share ways to reduce injuries and damage, from securing tall furniture to keeping escape routes clear.

  4. Help households make a plan

    Use the checklist and safety guides to help families decide where to go, who to contact, and who might need help.
